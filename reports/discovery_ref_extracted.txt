  Text: The construction industry contributes significantly to the degradation of our environment, due to the large amount of energy and carbon emissions associated with its operations. Over the past few years, there has been increased interest in using sustainable and eco-friendly materials as alternatives to traditional building materials. One material that has good potential for use in the construction industry is cow dung (a very common agricultural waste product in India) which has shown great promise for applications such as bricks, plaster, and composite materials.
