Regulatory Notice & Medical Scope of Practice

DermaNova provides algorithmic optical wellness measurements, computer vision chromatic mapping, and epidermal telemetry for longitudinal self-tracking. The clinician you consult through this portal is an independent, board-certified dermatologist licensed by the State of Washington Department of Health. Official medical diagnoses, cutaneous pathology assessments, and prescription drug orders are issued strictly and exclusively under the independent physician's medical discretion following formal clinical examination.

emergency

Immediate Medical Attention: If you are experiencing acute facial swelling, sudden severe blistering, signs of systemic infection, high fever, or shortness of breath, do not wait for an outpatient telehealth consultation. Please dial 911 or visit the nearest hospital emergency department immediately.
