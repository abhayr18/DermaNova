## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a serene, high-intellect aesthetic bridging dermatological science with mindful luxury skincare. It rejects clinical austerity and cold biotech diagnostics in favor of an editorial, warm-alabaster atmosphere that lowers heart rates and establishes immediate trust.

The target audience comprises discerning individuals seeking precise, computer-vision skin diagnostics alongside tailored botanical and clinical ingredient pathways. The experience must feel like a consultation within an architectural apothecary or an enlightened wellness clinic: tranquil, rigorous, confidential, and meticulously composed.

Visually, the language draws from high-end print editorial design and minimalist tactile surfaces. It balances ample warm negative space with precise typographic hierarchy, ultra-subtle ambient depth, and whisper-weight structural dividers. Visual tension is achieved not through saturated alerts or loud CTA banners, but through deliberate typographic contrast: an authoritative, humanist serif paired with ultra-crisp, rational grotesque forms.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in restorative botanicals, warm calcium tones, and balanced mineral neutrals. 

- **Canvas & Surfaces**: The base canvas rests on `#FAF9F6` (Alabaster Linen). Floating cards and elevated modular surfaces utilize `#FFFFFF`, while recessed compartments, scan viewports, and segmented controls sit on `#F4F3EE` (Subtle Limestone).
- **Core Botanical Chromas**: `#527665` (Herbal Sage) provides the primary brand signal for actionable states, key telemetry indicators, and confidence ratings. `#304D40` (Deep Forest) serves as the anchoring authority shade for structural headers, primary dark button surfaces, and high-emphasis focal items.
- **Supportive Tints**: `#E8EFE9` (Soft Sage) serves as light badge backgrounds, active pill highlights, and scan overlays. `#EEEAF5` (Muted Lavender) delivers a delicate wellness accent for hydration metrics and peptide/barrier indicators. `#F3E5E2` (Blush Porcelain) introduces organic warmth for sensitivity indices and micro-circulation markers.
- **Typography & Boundaries**: Main reading body copy is `#252923` (Charcoal Slate), avoiding absolute black to maintain paper-like warmth. Subdued data, metric units, and metadata rely on `#777D76`. Borders and hairline dividers are locked to `#E8E8E1` (Whisper Border), establishing subtle structure without harsh geometric slicing.

## Typography

The type system creates an intentional dialogue between **Newsreader** (the voice of heritage dermatological knowledge, editorial reflection, and diagnosis storytelling) and **Manrope** (the voice of analytical precision, machine telemetry, and interface ergonomics).

- **Headlines & Display Roles**: Set in Newsreader with optical sizing characteristics. Display sizes leverage italicized variants exclusively for emphasis on medical milestones, scientific nomenclature (e.g., *Stratum corneum*), or personalized skin attributes. Line heights remain generous to retain editorial elegance.
- **Interface & Data Architecture**: Manrope handles all operational touchpoints: form controls, diagnostic metrics, confidence percentages, and medical disclaimers. Its tall x-height and open apertures maintain legibility on high-density facial scans.
- **Labels & Micro-copy**: Set in Manrope semibold or bold with uppercase tracking on `label-xs` (+0.08em) for clinical parameter tags, AI confidence ratings, and privacy statuses.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a restrained 12-column fixed-max grid on desktop (max width 1320px) with generous 48px (`margin`) outer offsets and 24px (`gutter`) column spacing, allowing extensive breathing room around critical photographic captures and analysis reads.

- **Breakpoints**:
  - **Desktop (1024px and above)**: 12-column grid. Diagnostic tools feature a balanced 7:5 split (7 columns for high-resolution interactive face topography mapping, 5 columns for deep metric breakdowns and clinical regimen recommendations).
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column layout with 24px margins. Telemetry panels stack below the main scan interface.
  - **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4-column layout with 20px (`margin-mobile`) margins and 16px (`gutter-mobile`) gutters. Floating bottom action sheets host the parameter inspection tools.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Built upon a strict 8px base unit. Internal card padding leverages `space-lg` (24px) for desktop panels and `space-md` (16px) for compact list nodes, ensuring elements feel spacious yet interconnected.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on quiet ambient diffusion and tonal separation rather than sharp, heavy drop shadows. The design system rejects pitch-black cast shadows completely.

- **Card Elevation (Ambient Resting)**: White containers resting on the `#FAF9F6` background utilize a double-layer ambient shadow tinted with Deep Forest:
  `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(48, 77, 64, 0.04), 0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(48, 77, 64, 0.06)`
  Combined with a continuous 1px perimeter border rendered in `#E8E8E1`.
- **Active & Hover States**: Diagnostic detail cards subtly lift using:
  `0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(48, 77, 64, 0.06), 0px 16px 36px -6px rgba(48, 77, 64, 0.08)`
  with the border transitioning smoothly from `#E8E8E1` to `#527665` at 40% opacity.
- **Floating Modals & Inspection HUDs**:
  `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(37, 41, 35, 0.08), 0px 24px 64px -12px rgba(37, 41, 35, 0.12)`
  Surfaces leverage a subtle backdrop filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with 92% surface opacity (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`) for non-intrusive floating analysis loupes.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined rounded geometry (`level 2`) to balance organic gentleness with architectural structure.

- **Primary Cards & Telemetry Pods**: Standardized at `rounded-lg` (16px) to house imagery and data clusters smoothly.
- **Interactive Buttons & Form Fields**: Set to 10px–12px border radii for a grounded, tactile touch surface that feels more stable and clinical than an informal full-pill button.
- **Pills, Metric Badges, & Status Nodes**: Strict full-pill geometry (`rounded-full` / 9999px) is reserved exclusively for classification chips, AI confidence badges, and floating control pills (such as camera toggle controls and layer filters).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#304D40` background with `#FAF9F6` typography, 11px radius, 0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.05). Hover state softens to `#527665` over a 200ms ease. Padding: 12px 24px (`body-md` semibold).
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Pure `#FFFFFF` surface with 1px border in `#E8E8E1`, `#252923` typography. Hover brings an ultra-subtle wash of `#F4F3EE`.
- **Tertiary Link**: Borderless, Newsreader italic or Manrope medium with a 1px baseline underline in `#527665` spaced 4px beneath the text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Diagnostic Badges**: Pill-shaped containers (`rounded-full`) with a 4px 10px interior padding. Micro-text (`label-xs`).
  - *Normal/Balanced*: `#E8EFE9` background with `#304D40` text.
  - *Hydration/Moisture*: `#EEEAF5` background with `#4B3F72` text.
  - *Sensitive/Inflammation*: `#F3E5E2` background with `#7E4E45` text.

### Cards & Analytical Panels
- White `#FFFFFF` core surface with a 1px `#E8E8E1` perimeter frame and 16px radius.
- Cards maintain distinct header, body, and footer zones separated by faint hairlines or pure white-space breathing zones (`space-lg`).

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Text Fields**: Height of 48px, `#FFFFFF` interior, 1px border in `#E8E8E1`, 10px radius. Focus state swaps border to `#527665` with an ambient 3px outer glow ring of `rgba(82, 118, 101, 0.15)`. Text sits in `#252923` with placeholder text in `#777D76`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 18px rounded squares (4px radius for check; round for radio). Inactive: `#FFFFFF` with 1.5px `#E8E8E1` border. Active: Solid `#304D40` fill with crisp white vector checkmark.

### Analysis & Diagnostic Annotations (Domain Specific)
- **Face Landmark Callouts**: Thin-line concentric target indicators (1.5px stroke `#527665`, 12px diameter) anchored to an ultra-thin angled pointer line (1px `#527665` at 50% opacity) that connects to a floating white mini-card displaying the metric (e.g., *Pore Congestion: 12%*).
- **Privacy & Medical Notice Banners**: Encased within an `#F4F3EE` rounded container featuring an inline lock or stethoscope thin-line icon (Lucide style, 16px), accompanied by `body-sm` typography stating compliance (e.g., HIPAA-grade encryption, informational scope only).
