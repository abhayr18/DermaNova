Clinical Practice Guardrails

When to Consult a Board-Certified Dermatologist

check_circle_outline Normal Cosmetic Variation
  • Intermittent flushing triggered by exercise, mild heat, or seasonal transitions that resolves within 30–60 minutes.
  • Absence of pain, stinging, burning sensations, or papular surface eruptions.
  • Transient erythema that diminishes smoothly following ceramide-based hydration.
warning Clinical Referral Indicators
  • Persistent fixed malar erythema lasting longer than 4 hours without external heat stimulus.
  • Visible spider telangiectasias, stinging upon contact with plain room-temperature water.
  • Development of small inflammatory papules, pustules, or ocular dryness/stinging (suspected Rosacea).
